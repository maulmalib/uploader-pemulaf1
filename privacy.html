Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Uploader pemulaf1 ("the app") is a personal, non-commercial tool. Only the creator of @pemulaf1 uses it, and only with their own accounts.

Data the app accesses

  • Google / YouTube: permission to upload videos to the connected YouTube channel (youtube.upload) and to read basic channel information such as the channel name (youtube.readonly).
  • The video files, titles, descriptions, and captions that the creator chooses to publish.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to publish the creator's own content and to show upload results.
  • Login tokens are stored only on the creator's own computer, in the Windows Credential Manager.
  • A local post history (date, platform, caption, link) is kept on the creator's own computer.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or sent to any server other than the platforms the creator chooses to post to.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Meta (Instagram / Facebook): permission to publish to the creator's own Facebook Page and the Instagram professional account linked to it (pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts, business_management, instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish).

Threads: permission to read the creator's basic Threads profile and publish to it (threads_basic, threads_content_publish). For video posts, the file is made available to Threads through a temporary, random, single-file link that is closed right after Threads has fetched it.

TikTok: permission to read the creator's basic profile and to upload videos to the creator's own TikTok inbox as drafts (user.info.basic, video.upload). Nothing is published on TikTok until the creator posts it manually in the TikTok app.

Removing access & deleting data

Access can be removed at any time with the "Disconnect" button in the app, which deletes the stored token from the computer. It can also be removed from myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or from Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (Meta).

The app does not store any data on a server. All data (tokens and the local post history) lives only on the creator's own computer and can be deleted by removing the app folder. For any deletion request, contact the creator on Instagram (@pemulaf1).
