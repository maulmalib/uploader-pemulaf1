Uploader pemulaf1

Uploader pemulaf1 is a personal tool used by the creator of the F1 content account @pemulaf1 to publish their own videos and photos to their own YouTube channel and social media accounts from one place.

The app runs only on the creator's own laptop. It is not offered to the public, and there is no sign-up. It only accesses the accounts that the creator connects themselves.

What it does

  • Uploads videos chosen by the creator to the creator's own YouTube channel.
  • Shows the result and a link to the uploaded video.
  • Keeps a local record of the creator's own posts.
